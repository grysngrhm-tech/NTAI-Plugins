# Learners' privacy with NTAI

What instructors and TAs see, and what they do not, follows NTA's program policy. NTAI enforces it; this page says how to work within it.

| What | How NTAI handles it | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| A learner's progress | Shown by learning objective only: each practised objective's state and the day it was first mastered, readiness review results, the day they last practised, open practice sets. Never questions, answers, chosen options, chats, or when they asked NTAI about a graded question | Use it to teach. Do not copy it into other tools, documents or messages |
| Who the learner is | Looked up in NTA Connect from a profile link or email when asked; nothing about who they are is kept. Later calls use a sealed `learner_ref` | Give NTAI the link or email only. Never paste a learner's name or email into anything but NTAI's tools |
| Sharing | On by default; a learner can turn it off. Off looks exactly like no record | Never guess why there is no record, or press a learner to share |
| Views | Each view of a learner's progress is recorded and shown to the learner as the viewer's role and the time | Look when there is a teaching reason to |
| Group figures | Counts of learners only, never who; each figure needs a minimum number of learners, and smaller groups show none | Never estimate a hidden figure or work out who is in a group |
| Messages | `draft_message` drafts; NTAI never sends or keeps it | The instructor edits and sends it themselves |
| Practice sets | `assign_practice` adds one to the learner's NTAI home if they can receive it, only when the instructor asks; the answer is the same either way | Confirm the objectives and due date before calling it |
| Health information | Not asked for, not kept | Never put anyone's health details into NTAI or any AI tool |

When unsure whether something is appropriate, leave the learner's details out and ask the instructor.
