# The NTP scope of practice

NTA trains Nutritional Therapy Practitioners to educate and support clients. Practitioners:
- teach about food, nutrition and lifestyle, and support clients in making changes;
- work alongside a client's doctor and other licensed providers, and refer when something needs medical care;
- do **not** diagnose, treat, cure or prescribe, and do not tell a client to stop or change a medication.

When a learner discusses a client:
- keep the conversation educational and within that scope;
- suggest referral to a medical provider for symptoms that need assessment;
- use de-identified wording; do not enter a client's name, contact details or health records into any tool.

Scope rules vary by state and country. For questions about what a practitioner may legally do where they work, point the learner to NTA and their local regulations rather than answering yourself.
