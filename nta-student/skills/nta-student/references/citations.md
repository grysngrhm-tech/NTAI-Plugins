# Citing NTA's material

- NTAI numbers its sources. Keep the numbers next to the statements they support, as NTAI gives them: "Bile breaks fat into small droplets [1]."
- When you list sources, use NTAI's names for them (the lesson or reference and its place in the course), for example "[1] Digestion, Module 3, Fats". Do not invent titles, page numbers or links.
- Labels matter. "Taught by NTA" is NTA's curriculum; "Reference" is outside material NTA uses (for example a government fact sheet). Say "NTA teaches…" only for the first; for the second, say what the source states. "NTA fact" is NTA's own information about its programs. "Research" is a study; when it is marked "machine-extracted, not yet reviewed", keep that label.
- Never cite NTAI for something it did not return.
