# NTA Staff

For Nutritional Therapy Association staff. Adds a skill for finding what NTA's curriculum, program information and references say with NTAI's `ask` tool, citing it in replies, routing what needs a person, and keeping health information out of AI tools; and the NTAI connector.

Needs an NTA staff account. NTA's Claude administrators can make this plugin Required for staff and attach it, with the NTAI connector, to staff channels in Claude in Slack (`docs/v2/PLUGINS.md` in NTAI's repository).
