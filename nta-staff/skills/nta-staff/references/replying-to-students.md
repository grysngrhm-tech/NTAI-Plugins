# Replying to students

1. Restate the question in general terms, without names or personal details, and run `search`.
2. Read the passages. Keep only what answers the question.
3. Write a short, plain reply in your own words. Cite the passages by number while drafting; in the message you send, name the lessons instead, for example "See Module 3, Digestion, in NTA Connect".
4. Do not include staff-only or licensed reference text in a reply to a student.
5. If the passages do not cover it, say NTA's material does not cover it and, where useful, who at NTA can help.
6. Before sending, check: no health information, no diagnosis or treatment advice, no graded-work answers.
