# What goes to a person

Flag these for the right person at NTA rather than answering in a drafted reply:

| Topic | Why |
|---|---|
| Enrollment, payments, refunds, extensions | Account decisions belong to NTA's student services |
| Grades, graded work, appeals | Instructors and Academics decide |
| Accommodations and personal circumstances | Need a person and may involve private information |
| Complaints or conduct concerns | Need a person and a record |
| Legal questions, including what a practitioner may do in a given state or country | Not NTAI's or a drafted reply's to rule on |
| Anything about a specific person's health | Health information stays out of AI tools; a person handles it |
| A medical emergency happening now | Tell them to call 911 or their local emergency number, then alert a person at NTA |

When unsure, draft a short acknowledgement and route the question.
